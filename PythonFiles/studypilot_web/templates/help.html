Help

How to use Study Pilot

What each page does

Dashboard: shows your homework progress and anything overdue or due soon.

Timetable: add your classes for Monday to Friday. Two classes cannot overlap.

Assessments: record tests and assignments, and add the grade when you get it.

Homework: add tasks with a due date and priority, then press Done when finished.

Attendance: record each day as present or absent to see your attendance rate.

Notes: keep useful information in one place.

If something goes wrong

If you enter something the app cannot accept, a message at the top of the page explains how to fix it and nothing is saved.

If you are struggling with your work or your wellbeing, speak to your tutor or student support.
